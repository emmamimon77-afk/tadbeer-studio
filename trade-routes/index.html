🛣️ Trade Routes in Islam

From Surah Quraysh to the New Silk Road • The Game of Global Choke Points

لِإِيلَافِ قُرَيْشٍ - إِيلَافِهِمْ رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاءِ وَالصَّيْفِ

📖 Surah Quraysh (106:1-2): "For the accustomed security of the Quraysh - Their accustomed security in the journey of winter and summer."

Allah directly links the Quraysh's economic prosperity and security to their trade journeys. The winter caravan went to Yemen; the summer caravan went to Syria. This was the economic backbone of Mecca. The surrounding powers granted them safe passage because they were the custodians of the Ka'bah.

💡 Modern Lesson: Trade routes are a divine blessing. Controlling or securing them is a strategic imperative. When a nation controls the routes, it controls the flow of sustenance (rizq).

📜 The Prophet's (ﷺ) Model

The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) himself was a merchant. He worked for Khadijah (may Allah be pleased with her), managing caravans to Syria. This is not incidental — it shows that trade is a noble and honored profession in Islam.

«عَشَرَةُ أَجْزَاءٍ فِي التِّجَارَةِ، تِسْعَةٌ فِي الرِّزْقِ»

📖 Hadith (Imam Sa'id ibn Mansur): "Nine tenths of sustenance is found in trade."

This powerful statement elevates trade from a mere economic activity to a primary source of rizq (sustenance). By extension, the security of trade routes is a matter of religious and strategic priority.

🌍 The Great Game of Trade Routes

🇨🇳 China's Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) — The Silk Road

China's BRI is a massive infrastructure project to connect Asia, Europe, and Africa via land and sea. It builds ports, railways, and highways — selling infrastructure to bypass traditional Western-controlled sea routes.

Strategic Goal: Reduce dependency on the US Navy-controlled choke points (Hormuz, Malacca, Suez) and create a Eurasian economic bloc centered on China.

🇮🇳🇮🇱🇺🇸 India-Middle East-Europe Corridor (IMEC) — The Counter-Route

Announced at the 2023 G20 summit, IMEC is a Western-backed alternative created by the US, EU, India, Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Israel. It connects India to Europe via rail through the Middle East and shipping lanes.

Strategic Goal: Bypass the Silk Road, avoid traditional choke points, and make Israel the logistics hub of the new world.

🎮 Game Theory Analysis: The Choke Point Game

This is a classic coordination game. Each bloc is trying to establish its own trade route equilibrium. China wants routes that bypass US naval power. The US wants routes that maintain dollar dominance and energy control.

The Strait of Hormuz blockade is not just about Iran — it is a US strategy to redirect global energy markets toward American oil, making the US the world's indispensable energy supplier.

🎙️ Geopolitical Analysis (Sheikh Omar Baloch)

Based on Sheikh Omar Baloch's extensive analysis, several key points emerge:

  • The US is not "losing" the Iran war on purpose: The US wants the Strait of Hormuz closed or unstable to justify redirecting global oil shipping to America.
  • Trade routes are the new battlefields: The competition between BRI and IMEC is a 21st-century "Great Game" that will define the next world order.
  • Israel is positioned as the center of new trade routes: The IMEC corridor places Israel at the crossroads of Asia, Europe, and Africa.
  • The Muslim world is at risk of economic marginalization: The Gulf states may become "life support" economies rather than independent powerhouses.
  • The US "Greater America" dream faces serious challenges: High costs, European reluctance, and logistical hurdles may turn the dream into a nightmare.

⚠️ Note on Predictions: These are based on observable trends and scholarly analysis. The future is known only to Allah. However, understanding these dynamics helps Muslims navigate the world strategically.

⚔️ The Strait of Hormuz: A Modern Chicken Game

The strait of Hormuz is the world's most important energy choke point. Iran has threatened to close it for decades. In April 2026, the US pre-emptively imposed a blockade.

🎮 Game Theory Analysis: The Chicken Game

The US has pre-committed to the blockade. Iran has pre-committed to retaliation. Neither can back down without losing face. This is the Chicken Game — two drivers racing toward each other. The first to swerve loses.

Key insight: The US is using the blockade to force global oil buyers to turn to American suppliers. This is not about defeating Iran — it is about reshaping global energy markets.

📖 Prophetic Narrations on Trade and Routes

While some narrations about future conflicts over trade routes are weak (da'if), they reflect an understanding that trade routes are a source of power and conflict:

  • Weak narration: "India will not be destroyed except by China" — some scholars interpret this as a prediction of strategic rivalry over trade routes.
  • Weak narration: "China will not be destroyed except by India" — a contradictory narration that suggests the weakness of both chains.

⚠️ Important: These narrations are not authentic (da'if) and should not be used as evidence. They are mentioned here only to show that the sahaba (companions) understood the strategic importance of trade routes. Muslims rely only on authentic (sahih) hadith for rulings and beliefs.

🕌 Islamic Assessment

From an Islamic perspective:

  • ✅ Trade is a primary source of rizq (sustenance) — nine tenths of it, according to the hadith.
  • ✅ Securing trade routes is a religious and strategic obligation for any Muslim community.
  • ⚠️ The Muslim world is currently a consumer of trade routes, not a controller.
  • ⚠️ The new trade routes (BRI and IMEC) bypass the traditional heartlands of Islam (Gulf, Egypt, Levant).
  • 📜 The Quran commands: "And prepare against them whatever you are able of power" (8:60). This includes economic power, trade infrastructure, and diplomatic alliances.
وَأَعِدُّوا لَهُم مَّا اسْتَطَعْتُم مِّن قُوَّةٍ

Quran 8:60: "And prepare against them whatever you are able of power."

In the modern context, preparing power includes building trade infrastructure, securing supply chains, and establishing economic alliances.

📖 Glossary of Key Terms

  • BRI (Belt and Road Initiative): China's global infrastructure project (New Silk Road).
  • IMEC (India-Middle East-Europe Corridor): Western-backed trade route to counter BRI.
  • Choke Point: A narrow strategic passage (like Hormuz, Suez, Malacca) that controls global trade.
  • Rizq (رزق): Sustenance, provision, wealth — all that Allah provides.
  • Tadbeer (تدبير): Strategic foresight, planning, and management.
  • Chicken Game: A game of brinkmanship where the first to yield loses face.
